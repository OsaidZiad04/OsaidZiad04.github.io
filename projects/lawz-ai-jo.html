Lawz AI JO

A source-grounded Arabic legal information prototype for selected Jordanian labour-law material.

Lawz retrieves local legal evidence, produces an initial Arabic explanation, and returns backend-generated citations so the answer can be inspected—not simply trusted.

01

Problem, scope & my role

Turning legal answers into inspectable research.

Jordanian labour-law questions often require Arabic research across legal provisions and supporting guidance. A general chatbot may sound confident without showing which source supports its answer.

Lawz narrows the problem to selected labour-law material. The primary RAG path retrieves relevant evidence before generation, then exposes the supporting references in the interface.

Legal boundary

The system provides an initial AI-supported explanation. It is not lawyer-validated, not legal advice, and does not replace the official legal text or a qualified legal professional.

Osaid's contribution

  • Team leadership and cross-component coordination
  • Frontend development for an Arabic-capable, direction-aware interface
  • Evaluation framework and result interpretation
  • Cross-component QA and system integration
  • Final technical presentation

This was a team project. These responsibilities do not imply sole ownership of the backend, retrieval, deployment, or knowledge-graph components.

02

Architecture & workflow

Question → retrieval → grounded answer → visible citations.

01 · Ask

Arabic legal question

The user asks a focused question about the selected Jordanian labour-law corpus.

02 · Retrieve

Relevant legal evidence

Multilingual embeddings and Weaviate retrieve legal chunks, followed by a small reranking step.

03 · Ground

Constrained explanation

FastAPI orchestrates a prompt grounded in the retrieved material, including weak-evidence behavior.

04 · Cite

Inspectable response

The backend creates citations from retrieved chunks and returns them with the Arabic answer.

Next.js interface FastAPI orchestration Multilingual E5 Weaviate retrieval

The model is not trusted to invent or format citations. Citation data is created server-side from the evidence returned by retrieval.

03

Evidence & knowledge graph

Proof appears beside the explanation.

Evidence is part of the product.

The interface presents the concise answer, legal references, retrieved evidence, chunk identifiers, and technical retrieval details. A reviewer can compare the generated explanation with the context that entered the prompt.

When the available material cannot support an answer, the system returns an explicit limitation and clears unrelated citations.

Guarded secondary layer

Neo4j proof of concept

A separate Text2Cypher path explores legal relationships through validated, read-only Cypher. It remains experimental and should not be described as a complete GraphRAG or as the production answer path.

04

Evaluation & limitations

Credibility includes what the system cannot claim.

Evaluation framework

  1. BaselineFrozen held-out set with a deterministic BM25-only Top-1 comparison.
  2. Full systemThree RAG runs to capture variation across generated responses.
  3. Human judgmentLegal correctness, evidence support, citation relevance, and material hallucination review.

Current status: the framework is implemented and final human review is in progress. No final evaluation score is claimed here.

Current boundaries

What Lawz does not claim

  • Coverage beyond the selected legal corpus
  • Lawyer validation or production legal advice
  • A complete graph retrieval layer
  • A finalized human-reviewed evaluation result
  • A fully localized Arabic frontend
