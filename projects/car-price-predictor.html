01 / Problem

Used-car value depends on many interacting attributes.

A structured regression problem.

Vehicle price estimation combines numeric variables such as model year and mileage with categorical variables such as manufacturer, fuel, transmission, condition, drive type, body type, paint color, and state. The project turns that mixed feature space into an interactive estimation workflow.

Numeric

Year and odometer

Continuous and ordered values carry strong depreciation signals.

Categorical

Vehicle configuration

Manufacturer, model, fuel, transmission, condition, and body type expand the feature space.

Delivery

Usable interface

Streamlit exposes model inputs without requiring notebook interaction.

06 / Limitations

Listings are market observations, not guaranteed valuations.

Predictions depend on data quality and distribution.

  • Listing prices may differ from completed transaction prices.
  • Missing or inconsistent listing attributes can reduce prediction quality.
  • Market conditions and geographic patterns can shift after model training.
  • The project provides an estimate, not a professional vehicle appraisal.
  • No live deployment status or current prediction score is claimed on this portfolio.
